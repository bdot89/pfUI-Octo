🐙 pfUI Octo for OctoWoW

One addon. Everything. ✨

pfUI Octo is a free all-in-one interface for WoW 1.12 (OctoWoW), version 1.1.34, made by Bdot89. It rolls on loot by your rules 🎲, loots every corpse around you 💰, points you to the nearest herbs and ore ⛏️, shows every boat and zeppelin on the map 🚢, runs your auctions and mail 🏦, tracks every DoT 🩸 - and replaces 25 add-ons with one options window, a live preview on every page and no stutter in raids. ⚡

📸 Everything inside

🧭 Layout mode (/moveui): every frame gets a labelled box. Drag and snap, resize with the wheel, lock frames together, right-click a box for its options.

🎲 OctoLazy: rolls your way

One rule per item and dungeon: Zul'Gurub bijous and coins, Black Morass sand, Necrotic Runes, Ahn'Qiraj idols. Click a roll to change it, click a name to edit it; Need never goes on what you can't use.

💰 Auto Loot

Every corpse within reach, looted the moment the fight ends, with a filter for what you take and what stays.

⛏️ Gathering arrows

The nearest herbs, ore, fish and chests with arrows and yards, shared with your group.

🦅 Flight timer

A bar with every stop and the time left, learned once, shared with guild and group. 🚢 Boats, zeppelins and the tram show on the world map with arrival times too.

🏦 Auctions

Our own auction window: every page at once, prices from what's up now, shopping lists, deals and a gold ledger.

📬 Mail

The whole inbox in one list. Collect all goes through it in order and never stalls on marks, full bags or COD.

🩸 OctoDoTs

Every enemy with its debuffs, yours and the raid's, with real timers; a macro maker for multi-DoTting.

⏳ CoolDownManager

Your cooldowns fill the bars by themselves, with talents counted and a dozen glows when they come ready. Right-click any icon for its own glows, states, sounds and animations.

🧪 OctoConsumes

Lists per character and raid: warns before a flask runs out, one key puts the next one on.

🧭 pfQuest built in

Quest givers and objectives on the map, the tracker, the /db database with OctoWoW's own quests.

🧍 Unit frames

Player to raid with a live preview; click casting and cast bars on their own tabs.

🎯 Nameplates

Sample plates you click to change: auras, casts, threat colours, quest marks, range fading.

⚔️ Action bars

Button looks, proc glows, cooldown swipes, fading and end-cap art.

📊 Meters & threat

Details!-deep breakdowns of every spell, target and death, plus threat on your target.

💥 Combat text

Five areas you place yourself, with looks, crits, sounds and alerts.

🛡️ ItemRack

Gear sets that swap at once, a trinket queue and talent specs.

⏱️ Swing timer

Main hand, off hand and ranged, parry haste, queued attacks.

🎒 Bags

One window with categories, new-item glows and one-click sorting.

🗺️ Minimap & buffs

Shapes, buttons gathered up, buff timers.

🗺️ UI layouts

Normal, Raid, PvP or your own, switching by zone.

🔁 Octo or Blizzard

A switch for every part: back to the game's own frames at once.

🛠️ How to install

  1. Close the game ⏹️

    Add-ons and DLLs are only read when the game starts.

  2. Get pfUI Octo 📁

    • 🚀 OctoLauncher (easiest, updates itself): Addons → Add custom git addon → paste https://github.com/bdot89/pfUI. Check for updates keeps it current.
    • ⬇️ ZIP: download pfUI.zip, Extract All, put the pfUI folder in OctoWoW\Interface\AddOns. Replace an old pfUI: your settings stay.

    ❗ Remove an old pfUI first (OctoLauncher's Addons list, or delete Interface\AddOns\pfUI; your settings live in WTF and stay). OctoLauncher's own list has a different pfUI under the same name: don't press Update on a ZIP install.

  3. Run the DLL installer 🪄 (optional)

    In the pfUI folder double-click Install pfUI Octo DLLs.bat ("Windows protected your PC": More info, then Run anyway). It fetches the newest ClassicAPI, SuperWoW, nampower and UnitXP_SP3 from their authors, backs up the old ones and updates dlls.txt. Everything works without them.

  4. Play! 🎮

    Start the game from OctoLauncher. A short wizard picks your look; /pfui opens the options, /moveui moves frames.
